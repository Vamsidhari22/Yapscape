Your clients expect you to know first

It’s a big part of what they pay you for. The trouble is that the conversation about any one brand is spread across Instagram comments, TikTok videos, Reddit threads and LinkedIn posts.

Nobody has time to go through all of that for every client. So it usually ends up as a few screenshots in a deck, a week after it mattered.

Where it helps

Before a brief

Find out what the audience actually thinks, and the words they use, before any creative goes in front of them.

When a competitor moves

See what the competition is up to early enough to respond, or to get there first.

Once a campaign is live

Follow engagement and how people are reacting, so your report covers what they said as well as what they clicked.

How it works

Yapscape pulls in what people are saying about your client, their competitors and their category. It works out what’s actually changing, like a complaint that keeps coming up or a competitor trying a new angle.

Then it turns that into a recommendation you can take straight to the client.

Frequently asked questions

What does Yapscape track for a client?

Conversations about the brand, its competitors and its category across the main social platforms. It then picks out what’s changing and suggests what to do about it.

Which platforms are covered?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit, Threads, Facebook and LinkedIn. Coverage differs a little between platforms while we’re in early access.

How do I get access?

We’re in limited early access right now. Request access and we’ll get in touch with you directly. We start with a Yapscape analysis of your own brand.
