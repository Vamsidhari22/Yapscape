People talk about your products every day

Most of it happens in comments, reviews and threads that never show up in your notifications. A formula change people don’t like. A cheaper dupe that’s suddenly everywhere. A competitor pushing a new angle.

If you only find out in the monthly report, you’ve usually missed the moment.

What it looks like in practice

Say you’ve just changed the formula of your best-selling serum, and people start posting:

  • “the new formula broke me out, bring back the old one please” on Instagram
  • “anyone else’s skin freaking out since they changed the scent?” on Reddit
  • “trying the fragrance free dupe everyone’s obsessed with” on TikTok

Any one of these is easy to miss. Yapscape puts them together and tells you what they add up to.
