Privacy policy

What Yapscape collects, why, and what we do with it.

Last updated 11 October 2026

Public social data

Yapscape reads publicly available posts, comments and reviews on social platforms to understand what people are saying about brands, their competitors and their categories. We do not buy private data, we do not access private accounts or private messages, and we never sell what we collect.

Information you give us

When you request early access, we ask for your name and email address. We only use them to get in touch with you about Yapscape and early access.

Who processes it

Early access requests are sent through Formspree, which receives form submissions on our behalf. This website is hosted on Vercel. These providers process data only to deliver their services to us.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics, we will update this policy first.

How long we keep it

We keep early access details for as long as we need them to contact you about Yapscape, and delete them when you ask us to.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details you’ve sent us. To make a request, send it through the early access form and tell us what you’d like us to do.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we’ll update this page and the date above.
