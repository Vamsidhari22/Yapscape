Why TikTok matters

People use TikTok to find products now, not only to pass the time. One video can push a product, or a cheaper dupe, onto millions of feeds in a few days.

The real reaction is usually in the comments. That’s where people say whether they’d buy it, what they switched from and what put them off.

Why it’s hard to keep up with

  • Trends come and go in days, faster than a weekly report.
  • Half the conversation is in captions and half is in the comments.
  • Your brand often comes up in videos about a competitor or a dupe.
  • There’s simply too much of it to scroll through.

What Yapscape does with it

Yapscape reads public TikTok posts and comments about you, your competitors and your category. It notices when something starts moving, like a dupe getting attention or a complaint spreading.

Then it puts that together with what’s happening elsewhere and gives you one recommendation.
