What is Yapscape?

Yapscape is a social listening and marketing intelligence platform for marketing agencies and in-house teams. It gathers what people are saying about your brand, your competitors and your category, spots what is actually changing, and turns it into a recommendation tailored to you.

Who is Yapscape for?

Marketing agencies and in-house marketing teams: the founders, strategists and social media managers who need to know what their audience thinks before they brief, post or respond.

Which platforms does Yapscape listen to?

The platforms where your audience talks, including Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit, Threads, Facebook and LinkedIn. Coverage varies by platform while Yapscape is in early access.

What does Yapscape actually give me?

A clear read on your landscape instead of a feed to scroll: a rising complaint, a competitor’s new angle, a shift in what people want, and a recommendation on what to do next. For example: fragrance complaints on your serum are climbing with demand for fragrance free products.

When should I use Yapscape?

Before you brief a campaign, to know what your audience actually thinks. Before a competitor’s next move, to catch the shift early enough to lead it. And once a campaign goes live, to track engagement and how sentiment lands.

What data does Yapscape use?

Yapscape reads publicly available posts, comments and reviews. It does not buy private data and never sells what it collects.

How do I get access to Yapscape?

Yapscape is in limited early access. Request access and we’ll reach out to you personally, starting with a Yapscape analysis of your own brand.
