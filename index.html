What is Yapscape?

Yapscape is a social listening and marketing intelligence tool for marketing agencies and in-house teams. It keeps track of what people are saying about your brand, your competitors and your category, picks out what’s changing, and tells you what to do about it.

Who is Yapscape for?

Marketing agencies and in-house teams. In practice that’s usually founders, strategists and social media managers: the people who need to know what their audience thinks before they brief a campaign, post or reply. Read more about how it works for agencies and for in-house teams.

Which platforms does Yapscape listen to?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit, Threads, Facebook and LinkedIn. Coverage differs a little between platforms while we’re in early access. There’s more detail on Reddit listening and TikTok listening.

What does Yapscape actually give me?

Instead of another feed to scroll, you get a short read on what’s going on, along with what we’d suggest doing next. For example: “Fragrance complaints on your serum are climbing with demand for fragrance free products.”

When should I use Yapscape?

Most teams use it at three points: before briefing a campaign, when a competitor tries something new, and once a campaign is live and they want to see how people are reacting.

What data does Yapscape use?

Only publicly available posts, comments and reviews. We don’t buy private data and we never sell what we collect.
